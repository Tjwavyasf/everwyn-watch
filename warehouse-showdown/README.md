# Warehouse Showdown

A side-scrolling character action game for phones, set on a warehouse night shift. Fight through five shifts of thugs, gunners and brutes, then take on The Foreman in Overtime.

Open `index.html` in a phone or desktop browser. No build step or install needed. Play in landscape for the biggest view; portrait works too.

## Controls

| Action | Touch | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | ◀ ▶ | A / D or arrow keys |
| Jump (tap twice to double jump) | JUMP | Space / W |
| Slash (tap 3× for a combo, works in the air) | SLASH | J |
| Heavy (breaks a brute's armor; slams down in the air) | HEAVY | K |
| Dash (dodges through attacks; slash during a dash to lunge) | DASH | Shift |
| Parry (just before a hit lands; also sends bullets back) | PARRY | L |
| Surge (full meter: hits everything on screen) | SURGE | U |
| Pause / mute | ❚❚ / speaker | P / M |

## Tips

- Enemies glint right before they swing. That's your cue to parry or dash.
- A parried enemy is stunned and takes extra damage.
- Below 25% HP you go into **Last Stand**: you deal more damage and your surge charges faster.
- The Foreman's ground slam sends out shockwaves. Jump over them. After a charge or a slam he's **OPEN** for a moment.
- You heal 30 HP between shifts. Your best score is saved in the browser.
